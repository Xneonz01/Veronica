# Book club minutes

## 9 January

Present: Sam, Alex, Jordan, Casey, Riley, Avery and Rowan. We finished The Salt Road. Most of us liked the first half more than the ending; Jordan argued that the ending was the point.

Next book, chosen by vote: Winter Orchard, about 320 pages, so the next meeting is six weeks away instead of four. Casey has two copies to lend.

## 20 February

Hosts for the rest of the season, one each: Sam in March, Alex in April, Jordan in May and Casey in June. Whoever hosts picks the snacks, and the kitty pays up to 20 euros a meeting for them; receipts go to Riley, who keeps the accounts.

We voted to move from the first Tuesday of the month to the second Thursday, because two of us now have an evening class on Tuesdays. The vote passed five to two.

## 12 March

Winter Orchard split the room. Avery proposed reading one classic a season; agreed, starting in the summer. The kitty stands at 146 euros after this meeting.

Riley reminded everyone that the library's book-club sets can be borrowed for eight weeks, which would save buying copies. The next pick is The Glass Ferry.
