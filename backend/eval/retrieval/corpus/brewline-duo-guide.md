# Brewline Duo: owner's quick guide

## Setting up

Fill the water tank with fresh cold water up to the MAX line; it holds 1.8 litres. Slide it in until it clicks. Switch the machine on with the rocker switch at the back and wait until the ready light stops blinking, about 4 minutes from cold.

Before the first coffee, run two full tanks through the group head and the steam wand to rinse out what is left from the factory. Stand the machine at least 10 cm from the wall so the vents at the back can breathe. The drip tray holds 0.6 litres and has a red float that rises when it needs emptying.

## Grinding and dosing

The built-in grinder has 30 settings. Start at setting 12 for a medium roast. A good first recipe is 18 g of ground coffee in the double basket and 36 g of espresso in the cup, pulled in 27 to 30 seconds.

If the shot runs faster than 25 seconds, grind finer by two steps. If it chokes and takes longer than 35 seconds, grind coarser by one step. Tamp level with firm pressure; the magnetic dosing ring stops grounds spilling while you spread them.

## Steaming milk

Purge the wand for two seconds before and after every use to clear condensed water. Start with the tip just under the surface to stretch the milk, then sink it a little once the jug feels warm. Stop at 60 to 65 °C: milk scorches above 70 °C and tastes flat. Wipe the wand with a damp cloth straight away, before the milk dries on it.

## Cleaning and descaling

Backflush the group head once a week. Put the blind basket in, add 3 g of the cleaning powder, and run five cycles of ten seconds on and ten seconds off, then five more with clean water.

Descale every 8 weeks with soft water, every 4 weeks in a hard-water area, and at once when the amber DESC light blinks. Dissolve one sachet of citric-acid descaler in a full tank, run half through the steam wand and half through the group head, leave it for 20 minutes, then rinse with two full tanks.

## Error codes

- E01: the water tank is empty or not seated. Refill it and push it in until it clicks.
- E03: the pump has overheated after a long run. Switch off and let it cool for 20 minutes.
- E07: the boiler temperature sensor is faulty. Stop using the machine and book a service visit.
- E12: the grinder is jammed, usually by a small stone or very oily beans. Empty the hopper and run the grinder empty.
- E15: the drip tray is full. Empty it and slide it back.

## Warranty

The machine is covered for 2 years from the date of purchase, or 3 years if you register it within 30 days. Registration numbers look like BD-4471-K and are printed on the sticker under the drip tray. Keep the receipt: a service visit without it is charged at the standard rate of 65 euros.
