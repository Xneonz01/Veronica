# Lisbon, Tuesday to Saturday

## Getting there

Flight GW 1357 on the Tuesday leaves at 07:40 and lands at 09:55 local time. Bags are hand luggage only, 8 kg each. The metro from the airport to Baixa-Chiado takes about 25 minutes, with one change at Alameda.

## Where we are staying

Casa Azulejo, a small guesthouse in Alfama on a street of steps, so no big suitcases anyway. Booking reference HX-58213. Check-in is from 15:00; they will keep the bags if we arrive early. Breakfast is on the roof terrace from 08:00 to 10:30.

## Day by day

Wednesday: tram 28 early, before the queues, up to the castle, then lunch in Graça and the viewpoint at sunset.

Thursday: the train to Sintra leaves Rossio station at 09:11 and takes 40 minutes. The Pena Palace tickets are for the 10:30 entry slot, bought online; the palace is a steep walk or a short bus ride up from the station.

Friday: Belém by tram 15, the monastery in the morning and the custard tarts at the famous bakery in the afternoon, where the queue moves faster than it looks. Dinner is booked for 20:30 at O Farol, a table for four under the name Rowan.

## Money and practical things

Most places take cards, but the small cafés in Alfama prefer cash for anything under 10 euros. A 24-hour transport card costs 7 euros and covers the metro, trams and buses, but not the Sintra train. The emergency number is 112.
