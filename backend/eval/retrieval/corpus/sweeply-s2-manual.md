# Sweeply S2 robot vacuum: user manual

## First run

Charge the robot on its dock for a full 5 hours before the first clean. Put the dock against a wall with half a metre clear on each side and 1.5 metres in front, or the robot will struggle to find its way home. Open the app, pick "Add device" and hold the home button for three seconds until the light pulses white.

The first clean is a mapping run. Open every internal door you want mapped and pick up cables, socks and anything lighter than a paperback; the robot saves the map when it docks.

## Cleaning modes

Quiet mode runs the fan at 40 per cent and lasts about 150 minutes on a full battery. Standard mode is the default and lasts about 110 minutes. Max mode doubles the suction for rugs and lasts about 60 minutes; the robot switches to it by itself on carpet when carpet boost is on.

No-go zones are drawn on the map in the app. A zone keeps the robot out even when it is sent to clean the whole flat, so it is the right tool for pet bowls and the fringe of a rug.

## Maintenance

Empty the bin after every clean, over a bin bag rather than the kitchen floor, and tap the filter against the side to shake the fine dust out. Rinse the filter under the tap once a week and let it dry for 24 hours before putting it back; a damp filter smells and clogs. Cut hair off the main brush with the blade tucked in the brush cover, and pull the side brushes off to clear hair wound around their stems.

The wheels pick up threads and grit too. Once a month, turn the robot over on a towel, spin each wheel by hand and pull out anything caught in the axle; a wheel that drags makes it drift and miss strips of floor.

Replace the side brushes every 3 months, the main brush every 6 months and the filter every 2 months. Wipe the cliff sensors under the front bumper with a dry cloth every month, and the charging contacts on the dock at the same time.

## Error codes

- E01: a wheel is stuck or hanging over an edge. Put the robot on flat floor and check the wheels for hair.
- E03: the main brush is jammed. Remove the brush cover and clear what is wrapped around it.
- E07: the cliff sensors are dirty. Wipe them with a dry cloth and restart the clean.
- E09: the battery is too low to start. Put the robot on its dock.
- E14: the bin is missing. Push the bin in until it clicks.

## Battery and storage

Store the robot charged to about half if it will not be used for more than a month, and top it up every 3 months. The battery is rated for about 600 full charges; a replacement pack is part number SW-BAT-26.
