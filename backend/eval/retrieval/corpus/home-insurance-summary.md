# Home contents insurance: summary

Insurer: Kestrel Mutual. Policy number KM-HC-44871. The policy renews on 1 February each year; this year's premium is 214 euros, paid monthly.

## What is covered

Everything in the flat that we would take with us if we moved, up to 45,000 euros in total. A single item is covered up to 2,000 euros unless it is listed on the schedule; the camera and the laptop are listed, at their full value.

Accidental damage is included, so a laptop knocked off a table or wine on the sofa counts, not only theft, fire and water leaks.

## Bicycles

Bicycles are covered away from home up to 1,500 euros each, but only when they were locked to something that cannot be moved, like a stand or a railing, and not just locked to themselves. At home they count as ordinary contents.

## What is not covered

Wear and tear, mould and damp, damage done by pets, and anything left in an unlocked car. Losses while the flat is empty for more than 60 days in a row are not covered either, so tell the insurer before a long trip.

## Making a claim

Report a claim within 30 days through the Kestrel app, with photos and any receipts. Theft away from home has to be reported to the police first, and the claim needs the police reference. The excess is 250 euros per claim; the first 250 euros of every claim are ours to pay.
