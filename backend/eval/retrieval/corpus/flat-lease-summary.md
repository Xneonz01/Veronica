# The flat: key terms of the lease

These are the parts of the lease worth knowing without digging out the whole contract. The lease runs from 1 September 2025 for two years.

## Money

The rent is 1,150 euros a month, paid by standing order so that it arrives by the 3rd. The deposit was 2,300 euros, two months' rent, and it is held in the Safeguard deposit scheme rather than by the landlord; the scheme reference is on the certificate in the blue folder.

The rent can go up once a year, in September, by no more than the rise in consumer prices, and only with two months' written warning.

## Moving out

Either side can end the lease after the first year by giving three months' notice, in writing, by email or letter. The flat has to be handed back clean, with the carpets professionally cleaned, and the keys returned to the letting agent on the last day.

## Pets and guests

Pets need the landlord's written consent. Consent for one cat was given in March 2025 and is filed with the lease. Subletting any part of the flat is not allowed, but guests can stay for up to four weeks at a time.

## Repairs and upkeep

Report repairs to the letting agent, Morgan Hale at Riverside Lettings, by email. The tenant pays for small repairs up to 150 euros each, like a dripping tap or a new toilet seat; anything larger, and anything to do with the boiler, the roof or the wiring, is the landlord's to pay.

The landlord has the boiler serviced every October and sends the certificate afterwards. Smoke alarms are ours to test once a month.

## The building

Our parking space is number P14 in the underground car park; the gate opens with the small grey fob. Bins go out on Tuesday evening for Wednesday collection, and recycling every other week. The bike store is in the courtyard, and a space there has to be asked for from the agent.
