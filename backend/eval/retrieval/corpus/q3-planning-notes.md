Launch the new booking page by 15 August, so customers can pick a time slot without phoning. Cut support tickets by a fifth, mostly the questions about opening hours and parking that the help centre could answer. Keep the monthly running costs of the site under 400 euros.

Sam owns the booking page, from the design review to the launch. Alex owns the help centre, which means rewriting the twenty most read articles and adding a parking page with a map. Jordan owns the payment provider move and reports on it every Monday.

## Risks

The payment provider move is the biggest risk: the old provider switches off its old interface on 30 September, and nothing can be taken online after that if the move is late. The second risk is design time. The contract designer is free for only three weeks in July.

## Decisions
